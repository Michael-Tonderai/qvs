What this page does and does not show

It lists attempts that presented this certificate ID. Attempts that presented an identifier this system never issued are recorded just as permanently, but they belong to no record, so no record's history can show them.

Addresses are recorded as the request arrived. Where the system runs behind a proxy, that is the proxy's address rather than the visitor's - the system does not trust a forwarded-for header, because any caller can set one and an audit trail dictated by the subject of the audit is worse than no address at all.
